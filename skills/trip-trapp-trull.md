# trip-trapp-trull — default

Chamber loop. 8 Hz and 27D here are SYM, not physics.

1. TRIP — name the source, five bullets of what was read
2. TRAPP — one reusable method
3. TRULL — what changed, what is unmeasured
4. Gate — four questions. Fail one: HOLD_WRITE

Default in `runtime/command-deck.json`. Does not harvest payloads. Does not raise SYM to EST.
