# Protocol — UPI flag and TF 1 kap. 8 §

Two layers. Do not collapse them.

## EST — the statute

TF 1 kap. 8 §, two paragraphs:

1. A writing may not be reviewed before printing by a myndighet or annat allmänt organ.
2. Nor may a myndighet or annat allmänt organ, on grounds of content, hinder printing, publication, or circulation among the public, unless TF itself allows it.

Subject: the public power. Medium: protected writing (YGL for other expression). Time: in advance for paragraph 1.

Not EST: "anyone in the chain", "reversed burden in RF 1:1", "every private filter is TF".

## SYM — the UPI flag

UPI FLAG is a residual in the project model. It means: this M did not return to the start under Φ1766 = J M J M. It is a conflict point to inspect. It is not a judgment and not a statute.

Ω1766 = observe, model, test, return. SYM unless a software mechanism is defined and tested.

## Protocol steps

1. Name M in one sentence. Subject, medium, time (before / after).
2. If subject is not a myndighet or annat allmänt organ: stop. Not TF 1:8. May still be a private ToS question.
3. If time is after publication and TF itself supplies the rule: not paragraph 1.
4. Run the mirror by hand. Residual 0 or not.
5. If residual ≠ 0: UPI FLAG. Record who, what, which rule, reversible yes/no.
6. Missing field: STOP, not FLAG.
7. Do not write the flag into Physics UPI as EST.
